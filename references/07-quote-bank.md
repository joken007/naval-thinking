## 一、财富与杠杆

**1. 财富的定义**
> 求财富，不求金钱或地位。财富是睡觉时仍在为你赚钱的资产。
> Seek wealth, not money or status. Wealth is having assets that earn while you sleep.
> 《Building Wealth》

**2. 出租时间**
> 你不可能靠出租时间变富。你必须拥有股权——企业的一部分——才能获得财务自由。
> You're not going to get rich renting out your time.
> 《Building Wealth》

**3. 给社会它还不知道怎么得到的东西**
> 你会因为给社会提供它想要、但还不知道怎么得到的东西而变富。而且要规模化。
> You will get rich by giving society what it wants but does not yet know how to get. At scale.
> 《Building Wealth》

**4. 专属知识**
> 专属知识是无法被培训的知识。如果社会能培训你，它也能培训别人替代你。
> Specific knowledge is knowledge you cannot be trained for.
> 《Find and Build Specific Knowledge》

**5. 像玩一样**
> 构建专属知识对你来说像玩，对别人来说像工作。
> Building specific knowledge will feel like play to you but will look like work to others.
> 《Find and Build Specific Knowledge》

**6. 长期游戏**
> 和长期的人，玩长期的游戏。
> Play long-term games with long-term people.
> 《Play Long-Term Games with Long-Term People》

**7. 复利**
> 人生所有的回报——财富、关系、知识——都来自复利。
> All the returns in life, whether in wealth, relationships, or knowledge, come from compound interest.
> 《Play Long-Term Games with Long-Term People》

**8. 卖与建**
> 学会卖，学会建。两样都会，你不可阻挡。
> Learn to sell. Learn to build. If you can do both, you will be unstoppable.
> 《Building Wealth》

**9. 无需许可的杠杆**
> 代码与媒体是无需许可的杠杆。它们在你睡觉时为你工作。
> Code and media are permissionless leverage.
> 《Find a Position of Leverage》

**10. 杠杆放大判断**
> 杠杆是判断力的放大器。
> Leverage is a force multiplier for your judgment.
> 《Get Paid for Your Judgment》

**11. 没有「商业」这门技能**
> 没有一门叫「商业」的技能。避开商业杂志和商学院。
> There is no skill called "business."
> 《Get Paid for Your Judgment》

**12. 时薪**
> 给自己定一个理想时薪。解决成本低于时薪的问题，忽略它；外包成本低于时薪的事，外包它。
> Set and enforce an aspirational personal hourly rate.
> 《Prioritize and Focus》

**13. 世界最好**
> 成为你所在领域世界最好的人。不断重新定义你在做的事，直到这句话成立。
> Become the best in the world at what you do. Keep redefining what you do until this is true.
> 《Be Patient》

**14. 没有捷径**
> 没有快速致富的方法。那些只是别人从你身上致富。
> There are no get-rich-quick schemes. Those are just someone else getting rich off you.
> 《Be Patient》

**15. 真实**
> 靠真实逃离竞争。你和别人竞争，是因为你在复制他们。
> Escape competition through authenticity.
> 《Building Wealth》

**16. 终身学习者**
> 致富最重要的技能，是成为一个终身学习者。
> The most important skill for getting rich is becoming a perpetual learner.
> 《Building Wealth》

## 二、判断与决策

**17. 决定不了就是否**
> 如果你无法决定，答案就是否。
> If you can't decide, the answer is no.
> 《Learn the Skills of Decision-Making》

**18. 走上坡路**
> 如果两个选项势均力敌，选短期更痛的那条。
> Run uphill.
> 《Run Uphill》

**19. 清晰**
> 「清晰的思想者」比「聪明」是更高的评价。
> "Clear thinker" is a better compliment than "smart."
> 《How to Think Clearly》

**20. 讲给小孩**
> 如果你不能把它讲给一个小孩听，你就是没懂。
> If you can't explain it to a child, then you don't know it.
> 《How to Think Clearly》

**21. 打包信念**
> 任何你「打包接受」的信念都可疑，应该从基本原理重新评估。
> Any belief you took in a package is suspect.
> 《Shed Your Identity to See Reality》

**22. 智慧**
> 智慧是理解你行为的长期后果。
> Wisdom is understanding the long-term consequences of your actions.
> 《Rational Buddhism》

**23. 紧张与放松**
> 紧张是你以为自己应该成为的人；放松是你本来的样子。
> Tension is who you think you should be. Relaxation is who you are.
> 《Shed Your Identity to See Reality》（引自佛语）

**24. 读书**
> 读你爱的，直到你爱上阅读。
> Read what you love until you love to read.
> 《Learn to Love to Read》

**25. 读什么要小心**
> 你脑子里挥之不去的那首歌——所有念头都是这样运作的。小心你读的东西。
> You know that song you can't get out of your head? All thoughts work that way. Careful what you read.
> 《Learn to Love to Read》

**26. 自学的聪明人**
> 最聪明的人都是自学的，哪怕他们上过学。
> The smartest people are all self-taught, even if they went to school.
> https://x.com/naval/status/1880853934883197275

**27. 长期版本**
> 回头看，你会希望每件事当初都做了长期版本。
> When you look back, you'll wish you'd just done the long-term version of everything.
> https://x.com/naval/status/2105231982309564765

**28. 焦虑的解药**
> 焦虑的解药是行动。
> The solution to anxiety is action.
> https://x.com/naval/status/2013870666358087691

**29. 真相**
> 真相的问题是：你一旦看见，就再也无法假装没看见。
> The problem with truth is, once you see it, you can't unsee it.
> https://x.com/naval/status/2073737934851944481

## 三、幸福与平静

**30. 欲望即契约**
> 欲望是你和自己签的一份契约：在得到它之前，你允许自己不快乐。
> Desire is a contract you make with yourself to be unhappy until you get what you want.
> 《Every Desire Is a Chosen Unhappiness》

**31. 幸福是技能**
> 幸福是你做出的选择，也是你练出来的技能。
> Happiness is a choice you make and a skill you develop.
> 《Happiness Is a Choice》

**32. 满足与不满足**
> 幸福是对已有之物感到满足；成功来自不满足。二选一。
> Happiness is being satisfied with what you have. Success comes from dissatisfaction. Choose.
> 《Success Does Not Earn Happiness》

**33. 平静与幸福**
> 平静是静止的幸福，幸福是运动中的平静。
> Peace is happiness at rest, and happiness is peace in motion.
> 《Success Does Not Earn Happiness》

**34. 幸福的人**
> 幸福的人不是一直快乐的人，而是能不费力地解释事件、不丢掉内在平静的人。
> A happy person isn't someone who's happy all the time. It's someone who effortlessly interprets events in such a way that they don't lose their innate peace.
> 《Happiness Requires Peace》

**35. 三样买不到的东西**
> 平静的心、健康的身体、充满爱的家。这些东西买不到，只能挣来。
> A calm mind, a fit body, and a house full of love. These things cannot be bought.
> 《Building Judgment》

**36. 单人游戏**
> 人生是一场单人游戏。你独自出生，独自死去，独自解释一切。
> Life is a single-player game.
> 《Envy Is the Enemy of Happiness》

**37. 记分卡**
> 所有真正的记分卡，都是内在的。
> All the real scorecards are internal.
> 《Envy Is the Enemy of Happiness》

**38. 期待**
> 内心平静的敌人，是社会和别人灌进你脑子里的期待。
> The enemy of peace of mind is expectations drilled into you by society.
> 《Envy Is the Enemy of Happiness》

**39. 冥想**
> 冥想是心智的间歇性断食。
> Meditation is intermittent fasting for the mind.
> 《Meditation + Mental Strength》

**40. 回避**
> 我们大部分痛苦来自回避。冷水澡的痛苦，全在踮脚试探的那几十秒。
> Most of our suffering comes from avoidance.
> 《Meditation + Mental Strength》

**41. 愤怒**
> 愤怒是你手里握着的热煤，等着扔向别人。
> Anger is a hot coal you hold in your hand while waiting to throw it at somebody.
> 《Live by Your Values》（引自佛语）

**42. 越老越幸福**
> 如果你没有随着年龄变得更幸福，那是你做错了。
> If you aren't getting happier as you get older, you're doing it wrong.
> https://x.com/naval/status/2011358865187848389

**43. 浪费时间**
> 唯一真正被浪费的时间，是你希望自己在别处的时间。
> The only truly wasted time is time spent wishing you were somewhere else.
> https://x.com/naval/status/2065713308204974101

**44. 爱**
> 爱就是从另一个人的幸福里获得快乐。
> To love is to gain joy from another's well-being.
> https://x.com/naval/status/2092457056020414969

## 四、自我与成长

**45. 意义**
> 生命没有意义。你必须自己创造意义。
> There is no meaning to life. You have to create your own.
> 《The Meanings of Life》

**46. 诚实**
> 在你能对别人撒谎之前，你必须先对自己撒谎。
> Before you can lie to another, you must first lie to yourself.
> 《Live by Your Values》

**47. 价值观筛选**
> 你想离我越近，你的价值观就得好。
> The closer you want to get to me, the better your values have to be.
> 《How to Get Lucky》

**48. 鲨鱼**
> 鲨鱼吃得好，但一生都被鲨鱼围着。
> Sharks eat well but live a life surrounded by sharks.
> 《How to Get Lucky》

**49. 做东西的人**
> 做一个做出有趣东西的人。展示你的手艺，练习你的手艺，对的人最终会找到你。
> Be a maker who makes something interesting people want.
> 《How to Get Lucky》

**50. 承诺有意义的工作**
> 如果你不承诺做有意义的工作，生活就会用杂事填满你的时间。
> If you don't commit to meaningful work, life will fill your time with busywork.
> https://x.com/naval/status/1996857209628868659

**51. 长期游戏里互相变富**
> 长期游戏里，大家似乎都在让对方变富；短期游戏里，每个人只想让自己变富。
> In a long-term game, it seems that everybody is making each other rich.
> 《How to Get Lucky》（Nivi 语）

## 五、时代与职业

**52. 职业已死**
> 职业已死。工作在消亡。机会在出现。
> Careers are dead. Jobs are dying. Opportunities arising.
> https://x.com/naval/status/2024704647341232182

**53. 智力需求**
> 对智力的需求是无限的。
> There is unlimited demand for intelligence.
> https://x.com/naval/status/2020107233317515676

**54. 投资**
> 投资的秘密，是投那些不可阻挡的人，做那些必然发生的事。
> The secret to investing is to invest in unstoppable people building inevitable things.
> https://x.com/naval/status/1996774822949671416

**55. 别卖**
> 别卖你不想让自己孩子买的东西。
> Don't sell anything you wouldn't want your children to buy.
> https://x.com/naval/status/2081131954640969800

**56. 最该冒险的时候**
> 最该有野心的时候，是你一无所有或别无所求的时候。
> The time to be most ambitious is when you have nothing to lose or nothing left to gain.
> https://x.com/naval/status/2090680155618377834

**57. 努力工作**
> 只靠努力工作不会让你变富，但如果不努力工作，你也不会变富。
> You won't get rich just working hard, but if you don't work hard, you won't get rich.
> https://x.com/naval/status/2075804235191714160

**58. 男人与责任**
> 男人通过承担责任表达爱。
> A man expresses love through duty.
> https://x.com/naval/status/2058212450747715730
